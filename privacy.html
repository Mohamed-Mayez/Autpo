Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

1. Introduction

Autpo helps creators manage connected TikTok accounts, prepare content, store images, and organize scheduled posts. This policy explains how the Service may process personal information.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may process:

  • Account information, such as your name and email address.
  • TikTok information made available through the permissions you authorize, such as your TikTok user ID, username, and display name.
  • OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens needed to maintain an authorized TikTok connection.
  • Uploaded images, captions, hashtags, post details, and scheduling information.
  • Technical logs and error information needed to operate and troubleshoot the Service.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to provide the Service, connect to TikTok, manage your content, attempt authorized publishing actions, display post statuses, troubleshoot errors, maintain security, and respond to support requests.

4. TikTok Authorization and Tokens

TikTok handles its own authorization process. We do not ask you to provide your TikTok password. Authorization tokens are used to support the permissions and features you approve.

Tokens must be protected on the server, restricted to authorized processes, and excluded from public pages, source control, and application logs. The actual implementation must follow these practices.

5. Images and Scheduled Posts

Uploaded images may be stored on the application's server or hosting provider. Related post details and file references may be stored in the application database. This information is used to display, edit, schedule, and publish content at your direction.

6. Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with:

  • TikTok: when you connect an account or request an authorized API action.
  • Service providers: hosting, storage, database, and security providers used to operate the Service.
  • Legal authorities: when disclosure is required by law or necessary to address security or legal concerns.

Any additional analytics or third-party integrations used by the deployed application should be disclosed in this policy.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain scheduled content, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

You may request deletion of your account information, connected TikTok data, stored images, post records, and authorization tokens by contacting mn999663@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We may need to verify your identity before processing the request. Some information may remain temporarily in backups or where retention is legally required.

Revoking TikTok access does not necessarily delete information already stored by the Service. Contact us to request deletion of stored data.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. Access to personal information should be limited to authorized systems and people who need it.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may be entitled to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. You may also revoke TikTok permissions and stop using the Service.

10. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for individuals who are not permitted to use it under applicable law or TikTok's requirements.
